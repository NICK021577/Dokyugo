Resident phones now have a fixed Home / Bookings / Notifications / Profile navigation bar with the current location highlighted. Staff and administrator workspaces use desktop sidebars and tables. Staff call/recall queue numbers, open a PC phone dialer and exchange in-app appointment messages; administrators manage arrivals, walk-ins, service completion and settings.

Possible future work:

- **Verified SMS delivery:** verify mobile numbers, connect a provider and show delivery receipts/retries for reminders and queue calls. Current messages are in-app; `tel:` opens the PC's configured calling application.
- **Document review and release:** record requirements checked, approval, ready-for-collection and release, then add authorized printable document templates.
- **Resident language choice:** add English/Filipino labels and translated validation errors.
- **Shorter phone booking flow:** split documents, calendar and review into three screens while preserving selections.
- **Shared queue display:** add a dedicated hall monitor with large window/ticket numbers, optional spoken calls and no resident names or personal details.

The next useful release would pair verified SMS with document review/release, building on the arrival and queue timestamps already recorded.
