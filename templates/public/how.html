Common questions

Can I change or cancel my booking?{% icon 'Plus' 'how' %}

Yes, up to 24 hours before your slot. The place is then released for other residents.

What if I arrive late?{% icon 'Plus' 'how' %}

Please arrive at your booked time. Staff may mark your booking as missed after the 15-minute grace period. You can then book a new appointment.

Do I need a smartphone?{% icon 'Plus' 'how' %}

No. You can book using a computer, or visit Window 2 for walk-in service. Seniors and PWDs are served first.

Who sees my information?{% icon 'Plus' 'how' %}

Authorized barangay staff use your information to manage your request. Read our privacy notice for details.
